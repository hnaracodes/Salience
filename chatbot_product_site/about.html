About us

We sell subscriptions because ads ruin trust

Threadmind Labs was founded in 2024 by ex-infrastructure engineers who believed conversational AI should be funded by users, not by selling prompts to advertisers.

Our thesis

Free AI chat is a onboarding ramp, not a business model. Frontier models cost real money to run; hiding that behind ads leads to engagement hacking, dark patterns, and training on user data without meaningful consent.

We charge a fair subscription, show where the money goes, and ship features that make professionals faster — not features that maximize scroll time.
